# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in CodePilot AI to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent; critique code, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Credit others' work and respect licenses.
- Protect privacy: never post another person's credentials, private repository content, or personal data.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when recommending providers or dependencies.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory jokes or slurs.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Trolling, personal or political attacks, sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Encouraging or assisting attacks on systems or credentials.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This applies in repository spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report incidents confidentially to **[CONDUCT CONTACT EMAIL]** (placeholder — the operator must set a monitored address). Include what happened, links or screenshots where possible, and any immediate safety concerns. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers will acknowledge within 5 business days and may take any action they consider appropriate, including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal. Maintainers who are involved in an incident must recuse themselves.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. Correction — private warning with explanation of the impact.
2. Warning — consequences for continued behaviour.
3. Temporary ban — a defined period with no interaction.
4. Permanent ban — for a pattern of violations or a serious single incident.
